Last updated: 2026

=== DESTINATIONS ===
GOA:
- Best time to visit: October to March
- Top beaches: Baga, Calangute, Anjuna, Palolem, Agonda
- Must-eat: Fish curry rice, bebinca, prawn balchao
- Budget per day (budget traveler): INR 1500-2500
- Safety: Generally safe, avoid isolated beaches after dark

RAJASTHAN:
- Best time: October to February
- Top cities: Jaipur, Jodhpur, Udaipur, Jaisalmer
- Must-see: Amber Fort, City Palace, Sam Sand Dunes

KERALA:
- Best time: September to March
- Top highlights: Munnar, Alleppey, Kochi, Wayanad

=== TRAVEL TIPS ===
- Carry a photocopy of your ID and emergency contacts.
- Book trains 30 to 60 days early on IRCTC.
- Download offline maps before going remote.

=== BUDGET GUIDE ===
- Budget traveler India: INR 1000-2000/day
- Mid-range traveler India: INR 3000-6000/day
- Luxury traveler India: INR 8000+/day
